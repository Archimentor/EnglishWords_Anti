# 18단계 — 과거시제(Simple Past)

과거시제는:
> **과거에 일어났고 이미 끝난 일**
을 말할 때 쓴다.
---
## 1. 기본 형태
일반동사는 과거형으로 바뀐다.
### 현재
> I work every day.
### 과거
> I **worked** yesterday.
`work → worked`
---
## 2. 규칙동사
대부분의 동사는 뒤에 **-ed**를 붙인다.
- work → worked
- play → played
- clean → cleaned
- watch → watched
예:
> I worked yesterday.<br>나는 어제 일했다.
> She played tennis last weekend.<br>그녀는 지난 주말에 테니스를 쳤다.
---

---
## 4. 과거형에서는 주어에 따른 변화가 없다
현재시제에서는:
> I work.<br>She works.
하지만 과거에는:
> I worked.<br>She worked.
둘 다 같다.
불규칙동사도:
> I went.<br>He went.<br>They went.
모두 `went`.
즉:
> **과거형은 he/she/it 때문에 -s를 붙이지 않는다.**
---
## 5. 과거를 나타내는 표현
과거시제와 자주 같이 나온다.
- yesterday = 어제
- last night = 어젯밤
- last week = 지난주
- last year = 작년
- two days ago = 이틀 전
- in 2025 = 2025년에
예:
> I saw him yesterday.
> We traveled last year.
---
## 6. 부정문
과거 부정문에서는 **did not**을 쓴다.
축약:
> did not → didn't
구조:
> **주어 + didn't + 동사원형**
예:
> I worked yesterday.
부정:
> I **didn't work** yesterday.
중요하다.
> ❌ I didn't worked.<br>✅ I didn't work.
`did`가 이미 과거를 표시하기 때문에  
뒤의 동사는 **원형**으로 돌아간다.
---
## 7. 불규칙동사도 똑같다
긍정:
> She went home.
부정:
> She didn't go home.
> ❌ She didn't went home.
`didn't`이 있으므로 `went → go`
---
## 8. 의문문
과거 질문은 **Did**를 앞에 둔다.
구조:
> **Did + 주어 + 동사원형?**
예:
> You worked yesterday.
→ **Did you work yesterday?**
> She went home.
→ **Did she go home?**
역시:
> ❌ Did she went home?<br>✅ Did she go home?
---
## 9. 현재와 과거 비교
### 현재
> She works here.
부정:
> She doesn't work here.
질문:
> Does she work here?
---
### 과거
> She worked here.
부정:
> She didn't work here.
질문:
> Did she work here?
과거에서는 주어가 누구든 `did`를 쓴다.
---
## 10. be동사의 과거형
be동사는 일반동사와 다르다.
현재:
> am / is / are
과거:
> **was / were**
### was
> I was<br>he was<br>she was<br>it was
### were
> you were<br>we were<br>they were
예:
> I was tired yesterday.
> She was at home.
> They were busy.
---
## 11. be동사 과거 부정문
`not`만 붙인다.
> I was not tired.
> She was not home.
> They were not busy.
축약:
> wasn't<br>weren't
---
## 12. be동사 과거 의문문
be동사를 앞으로 보낸다.
> She was tired.
→ **Was she tired?**
> They were busy.
→ **Were they busy?**
`did`를 쓰지 않는다.
> ❌ Did she be tired?<br>✅ Was she tired?
---
## 핵심
### 일반동사 과거
긍정:
> I **worked**.<br>I **went**.
부정:
> I **didn't work**.<br>I **didn't go**.
질문:
> **Did** you **work**?<br>**Did** you **go**?
### be동사 과거
> I **was** tired.<br>They **were** tired.
---
## 연습
맞는 것을 골라봐.
1. I **go / went** home early yesterday.
2. She **worked / works** last weekend.
3. He **didn't eat / didn't ate** breakfast.
4. **Did you see / Did you saw** him?
5. I **was / were** tired yesterday.
6. They **was / were** at home.
